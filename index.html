How to use Vocal EQ Match

The AI changed the sound of your recorded voice a little. Your job is to move the sliders until the AI version sounds like your own mic recording, then save the result with a name. There are no wrong answers. Trust your ears.

Before you start

  • Use good headphones and a comfortable volume. Keep the volume the same the whole time.

Steps

  1. Pick the mic you recorded with: Flat mic or Podcast mic (left side, or top on a phone).
  2. Pick a recording. Start with Raw singing, marked Main.
  3. Press Play. Press the Space bar (or tap the buttons) to switch between Your mic and AI + your EQ. Both play at the same moment, so you hear only the difference.
  4. Move a slider up for more of that sound, down for less. Small moves of 1 to 3 dB are usually enough. Double-click a slider to put it back to 0.
  5. Press Match loudness so both versions play equally loud. A louder version always seems better, so this keeps the test fair.
  6. Check the Match picture. The flatter the line and the more it stays inside the shaded band, the closer the match.
  7. Try the other recordings. Choose All 4 recordings under “Changes apply to” to hear the same EQ on Baby cry, Crowd noise and White noise.
  8. Press Save EQ, type a name you will recognise (for example “Flat mic warm v1”) and your own name, then save. Each EQ name can be used only once.

Using a saved EQ

  • In Saved by everyone, press Apply next to an EQ and choose this recording only, or all 4 recordings on the mic.
  • You can apply saved EQs as many times as you like and keep adjusting. Save again under a new name to keep a new version.
  • You can delete EQs you saved from this browser. The dashboard owner can delete any of them.

Helpful buttons

  • AI original plays the AI with no EQ, so you can hear what your sliders changed.
  • Suggest a match sets the sliders to a computed starting point. Then fine-tune by ear.
  • Repeat one part: drag across the waveform. Press Stop repeating to play the whole recording again.
  • Reset to 0 puts every slider and the level back to zero.

Good to know

  • Both versions play at the AI's sound quality (up to 8 kHz), so you only hear differences that the EQ can fix.
  • Your slider positions are remembered in this browser until you reset them.
  • Saving asks for your name, which is shown next to your EQ in the saved list. Everyone with the link sees the saved list.
  • Keyboard: Space switch · P play or pause · arrow keys move a selected slider by 0.5 dB.
